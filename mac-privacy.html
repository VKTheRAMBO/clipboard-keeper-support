Clipboard Keeper Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026. This policy covers Clipboard Keeper for Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it has no network access.

What stays on your Mac

To do its job, the app saves text you copy, along with the date, time and the name of the app it came from. This is stored only on your Mac, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Your control

You can delete single clips, clear your history, pause recording, or choose apps to never record from. Copies marked as private by password managers are skipped. Deleting the app and its data removes your history.

Purchases

Apple handles App Store purchases. We do not receive your payment details.
