Clipboard Keeper Support

Questions or problems: worldlampapps@gmail.com

Why doesn't it save everything I copy automatically?

iOS does not let any app watch the clipboard in the background. Open the app and tap Paste, or use the keyboard, the Share Sheet or a shortcut.

How do I add the keyboard?

In iOS Settings, go to General, Keyboard, Keyboards, Add New Keyboard, and choose Clipboard Keeper. It types without any extra permission. To show your clips in it too, tap it and turn on Allow Full Access.

What does the free version include?

It saves 10 clips in total. You can delete clips at any time, but deleting one does not give a save back. A one-time purchase unlocks 1,000 clips, pinning, editing and CSV export. There is no subscription.

I already paid. How do I get my purchase back?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my data?

On your device only. Clips are not synced and are kept out of iCloud backups, so they do not move to a new device. Read the privacy policy.
