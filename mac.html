Clipboard Keeper for Mac Support

Questions or problems: worldlampapps@gmail.com

How do I paste a clip?

Press Control-Command-V, pick a clip and press Return. The clip is copied and the window closes. Then press Command-V in your app.

Why doesn't it paste by itself?

That would need extra permissions, so Clipboard Keeper leaves the final Command-V to you and asks for none.

Where did the app go?

It has no Dock icon. Look for the clipboard icon in the menu bar. Click it to open your history, or right-click it for Settings and Quit.

How do I keep it running after a restart?

Turn on Open at login in Settings.

The shortcut doesn't work

Another app may be using it. Open Settings and choose a different shortcut.

Does it save images or files?

No, text only.

Where is my data?

On your Mac only. Read the privacy policy.
